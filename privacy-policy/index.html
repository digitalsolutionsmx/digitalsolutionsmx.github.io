Privacy Policy for DigitalSolutions Apps

Last updated: November 2, 2026

DigitalSolutions (“we,” “us,” or “our”) develops the mobile applications listed below (each, an “App”). This single Privacy Policy applies to all of them. Jump to the app you’re using for its specific data practices; the sections after that apply to all of our Apps.

  • Team Generator (com.digitalsolutions.teamgenerator)
  • Digital Recorder (com.digitalsolutions.digitalrecorder)
  • Frequency Display (com.digitalsolutions.frequency)
  • Missile Strike (com.digitalsolutions.missile)
  • Chromatic Tuner (com.digitalsolutions.chromatic)

Team Generator

Team Generator lets you pick people from your phone’s contacts as “players” and randomly splits them into teams.

Contacts. With your permission (the Android Contacts permission), the App lets you pick people from your device’s contacts. For each contact you add as a player, the App stores their name, a reference to their photo, and their internal contact ID in a local database on your device, used only to display your player list and generate teams. This data is not uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party, and is removed when you delete the player in the App or uninstall it. You can revoke the Contacts permission at any time in Settings; the App will then be unable to add new players from your contacts, and photos may stop loading.

Advertising. The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and general device/usage information to serve and measure ads. Where required (e.g., users in the EEA/UK), a consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) is shown first so you can control ad personalization.

Analytics and crash data. The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for usage insights and crash diagnostics — see “Analytics and Crash Reporting” below.

What it doesn’t collect. Your contacts and player list never leave your device, and we do not receive or store payment card details. The App does not use your precise location. The optional free trial, described below, is the only feature that asks you to sign in.

Premium. The App offers an optional one-time “Premium” purchase (no ads and extra groups), processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Google handles the transaction, and you can review Google’s handling of payment data in the Google Play Terms of Service and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Free trial. If you start the optional 30-day free trial of Premium, you sign in once with your Google account through Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In). We receive your Google account ID, email address and name, and a hashed device identifier, and store a trial record (start time, account ID, hashed device ID) in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity are used to verify that requests come from the genuine app. This information is used only to provide the trial and to prevent it from being used more than once on the same account or device. It is not shared with anyone else. You can ask us to delete it at any time; see “Delete your data” below. You can use the App without ever signing in.

Digital Recorder

Digital Recorder is a voice recording app. It can also transcribe recordings on your device and, if you turn it on, back them up to your own cloud storage.

Microphone. With your permission (the Android Microphone permission), the App records audio you choose to record. Recordings are saved on your device, either in the App’s private storage or, with the default Device setting on Android 10 and newer, in the public Music/Digital Recorder folder (on older Android versions, in the App’s own folder). Their names, notes and transcripts are kept in the App’s database on your device. We never receive your recordings. A recording leaves your device only if you share it yourself (e.g., via Android’s Share menu to email, messaging, etc.) or if you turn on the cloud backup described below.

Cloud backup (optional). Backup to Google Drive or Dropbox is a Premium feature and is off until you connect a service in Settings → File. Earlier users who already had it on keep it. Once it is on, each new recording is uploaded automatically after it is saved, with no further action from you: over Wi-Fi only by default, or over mobile data too if you turn off “Upload over Wi-Fi only”. The audio file and its name go directly from your device to your own account. They do not pass through us and we cannot see them. Notes and transcripts are not uploaded. For Google Drive, you authorize the App with your Google account, and it can only reach the files it creates, in a “Digital Recorder” folder. For Dropbox, you sign in with your Dropbox account, and the sign-in credential is stored on your device. Uploaded copies are held by Google or Dropbox under their own privacy policies. They stay in your account if you delete the recording in the App, turn off backup or uninstall the App, so delete them there if you no longer want them.

Transcription. Speech-to-text runs entirely on your device: no audio or text is sent anywhere. The first time you transcribe in a language, the App downloads that language’s speech model from alphacephei.com, a third-party site. As with any download, that site receives your IP address and which model was requested, but no recordings, transcripts or account details.

Storage permissions. On older Android versions the App uses storage access to save recording files to your device. If recordings from a previous install are left in the Music/Digital Recorder folder, the App asks for permission to read your audio files so it can import them. They stay on your device.

Advertising. The App shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address (used to estimate approximate location), and general device/usage information to serve and measure ads. Where required (e.g., users in the EEA/UK), a consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads load so you can control ad personalization, and you can change your choice later in Settings → About Us → Ad privacy options. Ads are not shown with Premium or during the free trial.

Premium. The App offers an optional one-time “Premium” purchase (no ads, cloud backup, FLAC and 320 kbps recording, custom file names and app color), processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Google handles the transaction, and you can review Google’s handling of payment data in the Google Play Terms of Service and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Free trial. If you start the optional 30-day free trial of Premium, you sign in once with your Google account through Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In); no payment is needed. The trial cannot be started without a Google account. Firebase Authentication receives your Google account ID, email address, name and, if your account has one, a link to your profile picture. The App also creates a hashed device identifier (a SHA-256 hash of the Android device ID and the App’s package name) and stores two records in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): one with the trial’s start time, the time it was last checked and the hashed device ID, and one with the hashed device ID, your account ID and the time the trial was claimed. While the trial runs, the App checks in with the server at most once an hour. Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity are used to verify that requests come from the genuine app. This information is used only to provide the trial and to prevent it from being used more than once on the same account or device, so the records are kept after the trial ends. It is not shared with anyone else. You can ask us to delete it at any time; see “Delete your data” below.

Analytics and crash data. The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for usage insights and crash diagnostics — see “Analytics and Crash Reporting” below. The events it records cover app actions such as saved recordings, upload results and purchases, using fixed values and counts. They never include audio, file names, notes, transcripts or search terms. Where a consent form is shown, your choice also applies to Firebase Analytics; it does not change crash reporting.

What it doesn’t collect. No contacts and no precise location. We do not receive your recordings, notes or transcripts. You can record, transcribe and use the free features without signing in: an account is only needed for the free trial or for cloud backup.

Frequency Display

Frequency Display measures the frequency of the sound picked up by your microphone and shows it as a number, a musical note and a live spectrum.

Microphone. With your permission (the Android Microphone permission), the App reads audio from the microphone to calculate its frequency and spectrum in real time. Audio is processed only in memory on your device; it is never recorded, saved, or sent to us or any third party.

Advertising. The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address (used to estimate approximate location), and general device/usage information to serve and measure ads. Starting with version 1.0.11, where required (e.g., users in the EEA/UK), a consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads load so you can control ad personalization, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options.

Premium. The App offers an optional one-time “Premium” purchase (no ads), processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Google handles the transaction, and you can review Google’s handling of payment data in the Google Play Terms of Service and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash data. The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for usage insights and crash diagnostics — see “Analytics and Crash Reporting” below. The events it records cover app actions such as the microphone permission result, language changes and purchases, and never include audio or frequency readings. Where a consent form is shown, your choice also applies to Firebase Analytics; it does not change crash reporting.

What it doesn’t collect. No account or sign-in, no audio recordings, no contacts, no precise location. Your settings are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

Chromatic Tuner

Chromatic Tuner listens through your microphone and shows the nearest musical note and how many cents sharp or flat it is, to tune an instrument or your voice.

Microphone. With your permission (the Android Microphone permission), the App reads audio from the microphone to detect its pitch in real time. Your audio never leaves your device: it is processed only in memory, and it is never recorded, saved, or sent to us or any third party. If you use the optional noise sample, the App keeps only an average level and spectrum of the room’s noise on your device; it can’t be turned back into sound, and you can reset it at any time.

Advertising. The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address (used to estimate approximate location), and general device/usage information to serve and measure ads. Where required (e.g., users in the EEA/UK), a consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) is shown first so you can control ad personalization.

Premium. The App offers an optional one-time “Premium” purchase (no ads), processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Google handles the transaction, and you can review Google’s handling of payment data in the Google Play Terms of Service and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash data. The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for usage insights and crash diagnostics — see “Analytics and Crash Reporting” below. No audio is ever included.

What it doesn’t collect. No account or sign-in, no audio recordings, no contacts, no precise location. Your settings are stored only on your device.

Data Storage and Security

App-specific data you create (Team Generator’s player list; Digital Recorder’s recordings, notes and transcripts) is stored locally on your device. Frequency Display and Chromatic Tuner do not store audio at all. Missile Strike stores only your settings and game progress. We do not operate a server that stores your contacts, recordings, or player data. The only data we store on a server is the optional free-trial records described in the Team Generator and Digital Recorder sections, kept in Google Firebase. If you turn on Digital Recorder’s cloud backup, your recordings are also stored in your own Google Drive or Dropbox account, not with us.

If Android’s device backup is on, Android may also copy Digital Recorder’s private app data (settings, the recordings list with its notes and transcripts, and recordings kept in private storage) to your own Google account and restore it when you reinstall. Android and your Google account control that backup, and we cannot read it.

Uninstalling an App permanently deletes its locally stored data, with these exceptions: Digital Recorder recordings in the public Music/Digital Recorder folder stay on your device until you delete them, and free-trial records and cloud backups are not affected.

Your Choices

  • Team Generator: remove any player from within the App at any time; revoke the Contacts permission in Settings. The free trial is optional and you can use the App without signing in.
  • Digital Recorder: delete recordings from within the App at any time; revoke the Microphone permission in Settings. Cloud backup is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn Google Drive or Dropbox off at any time in Settings → File. Recordings already uploaded stay in your account until you delete them there. The free trial is optional and you can use the App without signing in. Change your ad-personalization choice in Settings → About Us → Ad privacy options (shown where a consent form is required), or remove ads with the optional Premium purchase.
  • Frequency Display: revoke the Microphone permission in Settings at any time; the App will then stop measuring sound. Change your ad-personalization choice in Settings → Privacy options (shown where a consent form is required), or remove ads with the optional Premium purchase.
  • Chromatic Tuner: revoke the Microphone permission in Settings at any time, or tap Pause to release the microphone; the App will then stop listening. Change your ad-personalization choice in Settings → Privacy options (shown where a consent form is required), or remove ads with the optional Premium purchase.
  • Missile Strike: change your ad-personalization choice in Options → Privacy options (shown where a consent form is required), or remove ads with the optional Premium purchase.
  • Where a consent form is shown (Team Generator, Digital Recorder, Frequency Display, Missile Strike and Chromatic Tuner, EEA/UK), you can also change your ad-personalization consent by clearing the App’s data or reinstalling it, which re-triggers the consent flow.
  • Uninstalling an App removes its locally stored data, except Digital Recorder recordings in the public Music/Digital Recorder folder, which you can delete with a file manager.

Delete your data

Team Generator and Digital Recorder free trials: to delete the Google account information we hold for your free trial (your account ID, email address, name and any profile picture link) and your trial record, email mxdigitalsolutions@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, or name that account in your message, and we will delete it within 30 days. Both Apps use the same sign-in, so a request covers both. Deleting ends any running trial. To prevent a free trial from being used more than once on the same device, we then keep only a record of the hashed device identifier and the date the trial was claimed, for each App you used. We remove your account ID from it, so it contains no account ID, email address or name, and it is no longer linked to you.

Everything else you create in the Apps (Team Generator’s player list; Digital Recorder’s recordings, notes and transcripts) is stored only on your device, unless you back it up yourself. You can delete it in the App or by uninstalling it. Digital Recorder recordings in the public Music/Digital Recorder folder stay after uninstalling, so delete those in the App first or with a file manager. Recordings backed up to Google Drive or Dropbox are in your own account, and only you can delete them there.
