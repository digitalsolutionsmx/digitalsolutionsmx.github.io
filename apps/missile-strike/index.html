Physics puzzle game

Missile Strike

Slide your launcher into place, set the angle, add some steering and launch. Your missile thrusts off the pad, curves toward the target and goes up in a big slow-motion explosion when it hits. Missile Strike is a physics puzzle about finding the perfect shot.

How to play

Place. Aim. Steer. Strike!

↔️

Position

Slide the launcher along the bottom of the screen, or drag it into place.

📐

Angle

From level 2-1, tilt the launch tube. A short preview shows where the missile heads.

↪️

Steering

From level 3-1, bend the flight into a steady curve to get around walls.

🚀

Launch

Watch it fly. Your last two shots stay on screen as faint trails, and you can line up the next one while the missile is still in the air.

Features

Every shot is a little puzzle

🧩

35 levels in 10 themes

  • Position and Angle: straight shots, then tilts past steel
  • Steering: curve around walls and into side pockets
  • Crates, Ricochet, Switches and Portals
  • Gravity Wells and Moving Targets
  • Final Round: keep your blasts away from your allies and take down the final boss

⭐

Stars and accuracy

  • Up to 3 stars: 50%, 75% or 90% accuracy
  • Hit 95% or better for a Perfect!
  • A level select shows your best stars, so you can go back and improve them

🌀

Unreal solutions

  • Think outside the box: on some levels a missile can leave the screen and curve back in
  • The view widens to follow it
  • Your Unreal solutions are marked in the level select

💥

Feel every hit

  • A fireball, shockwaves and burning debris in slow motion, bigger the closer you hit the center
  • Steering makes the missile roll and its fins spin
  • Sound effects for launches, explosions, bounces, crates, portals and switches

🎬

Learn as you go

  • Every theme mixes its new idea with the ones before
  • A short animated card shows each new idea before you play it
  • Run out of missiles on a theme you haven’t finished, and it starts over from its first level

⚙️

Your options

  • Sound, vibration and the animated cards can each be turned on or off
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device
  • No account or sign-in

Privacy

Just the game

Missile Strike needs no account and doesn’t use your microphone, contacts or location. Your settings and progress stay on your device. Full-screen ads never appear while you aim or fly.

Read the privacy policy
Do I need Premium to finish the game?

No. Every level can be played without it. Premium only removes the ads.

What happens when I run out of missiles?

Every level starts with 5 missiles. If you run out on a theme you haven’t finished yet, it starts over from its first level. Levels in a theme you’ve already completed can be retried right away.

What is an Unreal solution?

On some levels a missile can fly off the screen and curve back in to hit the target. The view widens to follow it, and the level select marks the levels you solved that way.

When do full-screen ads appear?

Only once you’ve played for a while, and only between levels, never while you aim or fly. Premium removes them, and the banner, for good.
