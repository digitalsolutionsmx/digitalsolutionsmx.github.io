Features

Everything you need to split teams

👥

How it works

  • Add players by name, or pick several from your contacts with their photos
  • Select who is playing today; your selection is remembered
  • Tap Make teams, then shuffle or swipe down for a new draw
  • Share the teams as text

🔢

Teams or players per team

  • Up to 9 teams, or up to 30 players per team
  • Extra players? Leave teams uneven in size, or send them to the bench

🔁

A fair turn for everyone

  • Queue: whoever has played most waits next
  • Tap Next match to bring waiting players in
  • Random: a new bench every time
  • Play 2 teams at a time while the rest wait

⚖️

Balanced teams

  • Rate each player with − and + buttons, on a scale up to 3, 5, 10 or 100
  • Strong and weak players are spread so teams come out even
  • Each team shows its average strength
  • Prefer pure luck? Leave strength off

🗂️

A group for every game

  • Each group has its own players, setup, team names and colors
  • Pick an icon, an emoji or your own picture
  • Duplicate a group to reuse its players (Premium)

🔒

Private and simple

  • No account needed to make teams
  • Players and photos stay on your device
  • Works without an internet connection
  • Light and dark themes, 49 languages

FAQ

Common questions

Does Team Generator upload my contacts?

No. Names and photos you add from contacts stay on your device. The app only reads the contacts you pick.

How does balancing work?

Give each player a strength. Balanced teams spreads strong and weak players so team totals come out as even as possible, and every shuffle still gives different teams.

What happens to extra players?

You choose: make some teams one player bigger, or send extras to the bench. With the Queue mode, whoever has played the most waits next.

Why do I need a Google account for the trial?

Only the 30-day free trial uses Google sign-in, so each person gets one trial. Making teams never needs an account. You can ask us to delete that data at any time; see Delete your data.

I bought “Remove ads”. Do I keep it?

Yes. Earlier “Remove ads” purchases are now Premium, and they are restored automatically with the same Google account.
