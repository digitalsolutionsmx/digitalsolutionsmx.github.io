Features

Precise, and easy to read

📈

What you see

  • The frequency in Hz, in large numbers you can read from a distance
  • The nearest musical note, for example A4 +0¢
  • A live spectrum with the main frequency marked
  • A level meter, so you know when the sound is loud enough

🎛️

Fine-tune the measurement

  • A4 reference from 415 to 466 Hz (440 Hz by default)
  • Minimum and maximum frequency
  • FFT size from 1024 to 16384 samples, with the resolution shown in Hz
  • Sample rate from 8 to 48 kHz
  • Silence threshold, smoothing and peak fall speed

  • Unprocessed (raw) source on devices that support it

Privacy

Private by design

Sound is analyzed in memory on your device. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded. There is no account or sign-in, and it measures without an internet connection.

Read the privacy policy
How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on the FFT size and sample rate. Settings show the resulting resolution in Hz, and larger FFT sizes give finer readings for steady tones.

Can it measure ultrasound?

It shows up to 20 kHz, the top of human hearing. What your phone can pick up near that limit depends on its microphone.

Can I use an external microphone?

Yes. Pick a wired headset, a USB microphone or a Bluetooth input as the audio source.

How do I remove the ads?

A one-time purchase in the app removes the banner for good, and it is restored automatically with the same Google account.
