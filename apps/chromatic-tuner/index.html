Features

See your pitch your way

🎯

Four ways to see your pitch

  • Gauge: a classic needle that settles in the green zone when you’re in tune
  • Strobe: moving bands that stand still only when you’re exactly in tune
  • Bars: an LED-style meter that’s easy to read from a distance
  • Trace: a scrolling graph of your pitch, to see how steady your note is
  • Switch between them with one tap

🎚️

Accurate and adjustable

  • Precise pitch detection that avoids octave errors on low bass strings and bowed instruments
  • Precision of ±50, ±25 or ±10 cents, or any range from 1 to 100
  • A4 calibration from 430 to 450 Hz (440 Hz by default), to match a piano or an orchestra
  • Pause button to release the microphone

🎨

Make it yours

  • 18 main colors, or pick any custom color
  • Hide the controls you don’t use for a clean, focused screen
  • Keep the screen on while you tune
  • Choose the app language

Private by design

  • Sound is analyzed in memory on your device
  • It is never recorded, saved or uploaded

  • Tunes without an internet connection

Privacy

Listens, never records

Sound is analyzed in memory on your device. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded. There is no account or sign-in, and it tunes without an internet connection.

What is a chromatic tuner?

One that recognizes all 12 notes, not just an instrument’s open strings. Use chromatic mode for any instrument or your voice, or pick an instrument to tune string by string.

How does the strobe display work?

Its bands drift one way when you’re flat and the other way when you’re sharp, and stand still only when you’re exactly in tune.

Can I tune to A4 = 442 Hz?

Yes. Set the reference anywhere from 430 to 450 Hz to match a piano, an orchestra or a recording.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Tuning never needs an internet connection.
