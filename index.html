Digital Recorder MP3

Voice recorder for MP3, M4A, WAV and FLAC, with transcription and silence trimming.

  • Record in MP3, M4A, WAV or FLAC, up to 320 kbps
  • On-device speech-to-text in 15 languages
  • Automatic Google Drive and Dropbox backup

Random Team Generator & Picker

Random or balanced teams for any game, with a waiting queue so everyone plays.
