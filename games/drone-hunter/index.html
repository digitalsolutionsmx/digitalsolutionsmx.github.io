Drone Hunter: Jet Dogfight

Enemy drones are heading for your base. Take off in an armed fighter jet, hunt them down and bring them down before they level it. Drone Hunter is a 3D air combat game of dogfights, missile locks and last-second saves.

Fast hunters that match your jet.

Each group ends with a black and gold boss. Later missions mix the drone types, and the drones learn new tricks: evasive turns, flares, radar decoys, tail guns and last-ditch dives into the base.

Features

Every mission: every drone down, base still standing

🎯

Lock, fire, break

  • Hold a drone in your sights until the lock tone turns steady, then launch a heat-seeking missile
  • Switch to the cannon up close, and watch the heat and your ammo
  • Boost to catch up, brake to turn the tables, and don’t climb so slowly that you stall
  • When the enemy locks on to you, drop flares and break away

💥

Damage you can see

  • Shoot off a wing, knock out a tail or set a fuel leak on fire, and watch the drone fall
  • Your own jet takes damage too, shown on a live diagram
  • The base loses buildings as it takes hits: shelters, tower, radar, fuel tanks
  • After a win, replays show your kills from changing camera angles

🕹️

Fly it your way

  • On-screen stick, tilt or game controller, with controller buttons you can remap
  • Chase camera, or a cockpit with a head-up display
  • Knots, km/h or mph, feet or meters
  • Three graphics levels to suit your phone
  • A short training flight teaches the basics, and you can skip it

🎨

Make it yours

  • Eight jet skins, from glossy navy to tiger stripes, or paint your own color (Premium)
  • Your progress, settings and controller layouts stay on your device
  • No account or sign-in

Privacy

Just the game

Drone Hunter needs no account and doesn’t use your microphone, camera, contacts or precise location. If you fly with tilt controls, the motion sensor is read only on your device. Your progress stays on your device.

When will it be on Google Play?

Soon. Drone Hunter is in Google Play’s review now, and this page will link to it as soon as it’s live.

Do I need Premium to finish the game?

No. Every mission can be played without it. Premium removes the ads, unlocks all jet skins and gives you the mission bonus without watching an ad.

Can I play with a game controller?

Yes. Use a game controller and remap its buttons, or fly with the on-screen stick or by tilting your phone.

Do ads ever appear while I fly?

No. The small banner appears only on the mission select and briefing screens, and the bonus ad plays only if you choose to watch it.
