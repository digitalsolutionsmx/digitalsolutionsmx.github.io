How do I restore a Premium purchase?

Install the app with the same Google account you used to buy it. Google Play restores Premium automatically. If it does not, open the app settings and tap Premium, or email us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund from your Google Play order history, or email us with your order number.

How do I delete my data?

Most data never leaves your device: uninstalling the app removes it. For the Team Generator trial account, see Delete your data in our privacy policy.

Do you build custom apps?

Send us an email describing your project and we will let you know if we can help.
